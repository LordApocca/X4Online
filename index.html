Requirements and limits

The Steam version of X4: Foundations 9.00 on Windows, the same DLCs as the host, the X4 Online app and a free account. Only the Steam version is supported: copies from GOG or the Epic Games Store won't work with X4 Online.

X4 doesn't let mods connect to the internet, so the app does that part: it links your game with the other players and the X4 Online server.

It also handles the save data that multiplayer needs, which X4 can't do by itself. An X4 save holds only one player, but a shared universe has several, each with their own progress. The app keeps every player's own map, encyclopedia, blueprints, inventory and stats alongside the host's save. When you join, it merges your progress into the host's universe before your game loads it, and when you leave, it takes your progress back out. It moves that save between the players' PCs and removes your copy once it's loaded, so everyone always plays the one shared universe.

The app also installs and updates the mod, handles your account and the server list, and starts X4 through Steam. It runs only on your PC while you play, and touches nothing besides its own files, the mod in X4's folder, the saves it handles for you, and X4 settings you agree to change.

Your account is how a shared universe knows which empire is yours. When you join, the host's game recognises your account and puts you back in your own faction, with your ships, stations and money, from whichever PC you play on, and nobody else can take your place.

It also keeps your faction's name, description, look and starting system, and gives you the server list, private sessions and chat. It's free, and all it asks for is an account name and a password: no email address.
